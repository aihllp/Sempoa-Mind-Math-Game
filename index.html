How Integer Cancellation Works in Mind Math:

Every White Bead (+1) combined with a Red Bead (-1) creates a Zero Pair. When they slide to the middle separator, they cancel each other out ($1 + (-1) = 0$). The final count remaining on the rod gives the answer!
